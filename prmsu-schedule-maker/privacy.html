Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

PRMSU Schedule Maker ("the tool") is a free, browser-based tool that turns a Certificate of Registration (COR) into a weekly class schedule image. Your privacy matters, so the tool is built to keep your data on your own device.

1. Your COR never leaves your device

Everything that involves your COR happens locally in your web browser. When you upload a COR, the file is read and processed on your device. The file and the information inside it (your name, student number, subjects, schedule, and so on) are never uploaded to any server. We have no accounts, and we never receive your COR or the schedule you generate.

2. The one thing we do store: anonymous counters

The page shows a shared hearts (likes) count and a visits count, so students can see the tool is used and appreciated. If you tell us your campus in the welcome popup, we add one to that campus's tally (and, if you heart the tool, one heart toward that campus too). That campus tally is shown publicly on the page as a simple "which campus uses this most" list, for transparency. These are just plain numbers. They hold no personal information: no names, no student numbers, no COR data, no accounts, and nothing that identifies you or your device. There is no way for anyone, including us, to see who answered; only the count per campus.

3. No tracking

The tool does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or any cross-site tracking technology to identify you. It does keep a few small flags in your browser's own local storage: to remember that you already liked the tool or were already counted as a visit (so your taps are not double-counted), that you have already seen the welcome popup, which campus you picked, and the most recent shared counts it saw. Those flags stay on your device and identify nothing about you.

4. Third-party services

To work, the page loads a few open-source libraries and fonts from public Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): Tesseract.js, PDF.js, html2canvas, and Google Fonts. The shared hearts and visits counter is stored with Supabase, so your browser sends those anonymous counter requests to supabase.co. As with any normal web request, these may include standard technical information (like your IP address and browser type). None of your COR content is ever sent to any of them. Please refer to those providers' own privacy policies for details.

5. The image you save

When you choose "Save as JPEG", the schedule image is generated in your browser and downloaded straight to your device. We never receive a copy.

6. Your responsibility

Because processing is local, you are in control of your files. Only upload a COR that belongs to you, or that you have permission to use. Keep saved images secure if they contain personal details.

7. Not affiliated with PRMSU

This is an independent, unofficial tool. It is not operated, endorsed, or affiliated with President Ramon Magsaysay State University. The university seal is shown only to identify the school on the generated schedule.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the creator, Red Pogi Lang.
